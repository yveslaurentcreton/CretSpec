## Small tool. Clear boundaries.

CretSpec is a native command-line tool for Windows, macOS and Linux. It prepares your workspace and checks the connections between its repositories. Your specs remain readable Markdown and JSON.

Local instructions and skills bring that context to your coding agent. Keep project skills in the spec, improve shared skills in your AI guidelines, and share changes with your normal Git workflow. [Explore the agent workflow](/CretSpec/guides/agents/).

No application server, account or project registry to maintain. Git handles access to your repositories. Your team decides what goes into its guidelines.

<p class="footnote">Native downloads are available. See the <a href="/CretSpec/start/install/">installation guide</a> for your platform and package-manager availability.</p>
