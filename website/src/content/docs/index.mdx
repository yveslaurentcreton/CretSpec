## Small tool. Clear boundaries.

CretSpec is a native command-line tool for Windows, macOS and Linux. It prepares your workspace and checks the connections between its repositories. Your specs remain readable Markdown and JSON.

No application server, account or project registry to maintain. Git handles access to your repositories. Your team decides what goes into its guidelines.
